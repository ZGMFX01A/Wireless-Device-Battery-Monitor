## 新增功能

- 支持读取 Windows 经典蓝牙耳机通过 Hands-Free（HFP）提供的电量信息。
- 统一管理经典蓝牙与 BLE 蓝牙设备的电量信息。
- 改善多蓝牙端点设备的识别，减少重复显示。

## 体验优化

- 优化设备重新连接后的绑定恢复。
- 设置面板支持折叠与展开，检测到设备后可集中查看电量信息。

<!-- lang:en -->
## New Features

- Added battery reporting for Classic Bluetooth headsets through Windows Hands-Free (HFP).
- Unified battery information handling for Classic Bluetooth and BLE devices.
- Improved recognition of devices with multiple Bluetooth endpoints to reduce duplicate entries.

## Experience Improvements

- Improved binding restoration after device reconnection.
- Added a collapsible Settings panel so battery information can take focus after devices are detected.

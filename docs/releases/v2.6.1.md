## 问题修复
- 修复添加和绑定蓝牙设备时因后台争抢硬件互斥锁导致的界面卡顿甚至超时失败问题。
- 修复状态同步在多线程并发场景下的数据冲突隐患，保证多设备状态更新的稳定性。
- 修复打开添加设备弹窗时偶发卡在加载状态的问题，支持多次请求安全合并。
- 修复移除蓝牙设备在后台超时未响应时界面状态与底层脱节的问题，增加超时明确提示与自动回滚。

## 体验优化
- 优化蓝牙设备添加交互：选择设备并点击添加后弹窗立即关闭，主界面卡片即时显示并于后台静默同步电量，无需停留在弹窗中等待。
- 优化蓝牙设备移除交互：确认移除后卡片瞬间隐去并给出即时提示，无需等待后台处理延迟。
- 优化手动刷新电量反馈：点击“刷新电量”后立即提供操作中与完成提示，界面响应更加明确。
- 优化设置窗口启动性能：打开窗口时后台静默预加载已配对蓝牙设备，缩短添加弹窗的等待时间。

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where adding and binding Bluetooth devices could stutter or time out due to background hardware lock contention.
- Fixed potential state synchronization conflicts under multithreaded concurrency, ensuring reliable multi-device status updates.
- Fixed an issue where the Bluetooth device picker dialog could occasionally get stuck in a loading state by properly coalescing concurrent scan requests.
- Fixed an issue where removing a Bluetooth device could cause UI state mismatch if the background timed out, adding clear timeout notifications and automatic rollback.

## Experience Improvements
- Improved Bluetooth device pairing experience: the dialog closes immediately upon adding a device, and the device card appears on the main screen with background battery sync, eliminating modal wait times.
- Improved Bluetooth device removal experience: confirmed removals now hide the device card instantly with immediate feedback, removing perceptible delays.
- Improved manual battery refresh feedback: clicking "Refresh Battery" now provides immediate in-progress and completion notifications for clear responsiveness.
- Improved settings window responsiveness: paired Bluetooth devices are now preloaded silently in the background when opening the window, reducing picker load times.
